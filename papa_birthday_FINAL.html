A LETTER FROM ME TO YOU

One Last Thing... 💌

Dear Papa,

Happy birthday! 🤍 I wanted to make something a little different for you this year, so I made this small website—not because a website could ever be enough to say everything I want to say, but because I wanted to give you something made especially for you.

Thank you for everything you do for me and for our family. I know there are so many things you do every day that you don't expect us to notice, but I hope you know that they matter. Your hard work, your sacrifices, the times you take care of us, and even the simple little moments we share are things I am truly grateful for.

Looking through our pictures while making this made me realize just how many memories we already have together. Some are big moments, some are completely ordinary days, but somehow those are the memories I want to keep the most. I hope we get to make so many more of them together.

I may not always say "thank you" or "I love you" as much as I should. Sometimes I might be quiet, busy, or simply not know how to say it. But please know that I appreciate you more than I can put into words. I'm really lucky to have you as my Papa, and I will always be grateful for everything you've done and continue to do for us.

On your birthday, I hope you get the happiness, peace, good health, and love that you deserve. I hope you have many more birthdays ahead of you, and I hope that when you look back on the years to come, you'll have even more happy memories with all of us.

Thank you for being there, for working hard, for making memories with us, and simply for being you. No matter how old I get, I will always be your daughter, and you will always be my Papa.

With all my love,
Your daughter 🤍
